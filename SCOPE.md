# Scope, epistemic status, and boundaries

**Status:** speculative conceptual research notebook. No implemented RSI system is claimed. No novel physical phenomenon is claimed. No claim of consciousness, sentience, or future AI behavior is implied.

## Purpose

Explore whether recursive self-improvement (RSI) is too often framed as optimization inside an already-specified space of models, algorithms, architectures, capabilities, and evaluators. Investigate cases in which the space of describable states, permitted transformations, or relevant comparison criteria changes as part of the process.

This repository is an invitation to formulate and criticize hypotheses, not a blueprint for deploying autonomous self-modifying systems.

## Distinctions we must not collapse

1. **Metaphor:** an imaginative device that exposes assumptions. The pulsing ball and its rings are metaphors.
2. **Formal candidate:** a mathematical object proposed to represent part of a metaphor. A formal candidate may be interesting even if it misrepresents the original intuition.
3. **Known connection:** a relationship to published mathematics, computer science, biology, or physics. Similarity is not derivation.
4. **Testable hypothesis:** a prediction with an operational measure, comparison class, and conditions for failure.
5. **Demonstrated result:** a result supported by a documented experiment within its stated scope.
6. **Extrapolation:** a claim extending beyond what the evidence currently supports.

These labels can coexist in one document, but claims should be marked.

## Invariants of intellectual hygiene

- **Molting is not improvement.** Transitions may lose abilities or destroy comparability.
- **Novel representation is not novel computability.** Distinguish theoretical computability from practical accessibility under constrained resources.
- **A changing coordinate description is not automatically a changing ontology.**
- **Failure of a learner is not proof that a phenomenon is intrinsically unlearnable.**
- **Absence of a useful global description is not proof that no global description exists.**
- **Self-modification is not authorization.** Discovery of useful modifications does not grant a system permission to enact them.
- **Continuity is an open question, not a presumed identity.**
- **Examples and diagrams are not implementation instructions.**

## Research style

Prefer: "Under assumptions A–C, what changes if D is not fixed?" over "This is how intelligence must work."

Use a recurring template for speculative notes:
- Provocation
- Fixed assumption being challenged
- Minimal example or thought experiment
- Candidate formal languages
- Observable consequence (if any)
- Competing interpretations
- Strongest objection
- Status / next unresolved question
