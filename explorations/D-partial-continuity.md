# D. Partial continuity without a global comparison map

**Status:** open research direction. Conceptual.

## Provocation

Can there be meaningful continuity across successive regimes when no single map translates every operation, observation, or success criterion from one regime to the next?

## Candidate observations

- Some causal dependencies or external test outcomes remain interpretable.
- Some recipes or witnesses can be partially reconstructed.
- Composition of successive partial maps fails (non-transitive continuity).
- Information-theoretic or rate-distortion costs of reconstruction grow.

Category-theoretic language (functors between categories of regimes, or spans/partial morphisms) can describe families of partial translations. It does not, by itself, guarantee that any particular external property is preserved or that the translations are computationally cheap.

## Sol's constraint

Do not assume a global metric or a permanently available comparison functor. Any concrete proposal must state what is mapped, what is lost, and what external witness is used to check the claim.

## Open

No worked example of non-transitive partial continuity has been added. The direction is available for a small formal sketch that includes an explicit failure of transitivity.
