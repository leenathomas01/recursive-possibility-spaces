# Research log: questions and attempted falsifications

**Initial entry: 2026-11-04.** A live notebook. Questions may be revised, retired, or replaced without implying a research program has been completed.

## Q01: Is molting more than a coordinate change?

Try to redescribe every proposed molt inside a single fixed meta-state space. If the redescription preserves all predictions and costs less, prefer it. If the redescription loses practical accessibility or intervention relevance, say exactly where.

## Q02: Can there be continuity without translation?

Specify a before/after witness (persistent memory, action success, causal dependence, reconstructable history). Test whether the witness survives when the representation changes. Never infer a persistent 'self' just because some information survives.

## Q03: Can a local trajectory be used without an accurate global model?

Design paired environments with identical sensory and compute budgets. Compare model-building, tracking, and local intervention strategies on outcomes and resources. Look for regimes where local action helps, and regimes where it catastrophically fails.

## Q04: Is 'operation-space expansion' genuine?

Require a novel operation to enable a held-out capability under equal total computation, including invention, evaluation, translation, and deployment cost. Compare against a baseline that compiles the operation into conventional code.

## Q05: Is the stability of a structure necessary for all kinds of useful interaction?

Differentiate supervised prediction, cancellation/control, opportunistic intervention, and robust abstention. A target may be impossible to identify globally but still support bounded actions; conversely, action may be hopeless even if local patterns are visible.

## Q06: Can recursive molting be evaluated without fixed goals?

If evaluation is permitted to change, ask what anchors nontrivial 'improvement' claims. Explore external held-out tasks, invariant resource constraints, Pareto comparisons, and human-specified approval boundaries. None alone may be sufficient.

## Q07: What does a shed ring do?

Three possibilities: historical trace; still-active constraint/interface; misleading observational artifact. Propose an observable way to tell them apart.

## Q08: Does the pulse cause the transformation or only reveal it?

Separate an observation, a perturbation, and an intervention. In causal experiments, record what changed even if no observation occurred.

## Q09: Could recursion stagnate, collapse, or cycle?

Build examples of successive representational transformations with declining generalization, unreachable earlier competencies, or periodic rediscovery of old structures. Seek conditions under which 'molting' is demonstrably not progress.

## Q10: What assumptions come from the observer?

Keep a running inventory: fixed objective, centralized agent, global clock, unified state vector, sequential improvement, differentiable loss, persistent identity, explicit symbolic representation, stable benchmark, and the observer's own categories.

## What has *not* been established

No RSI has been demonstrated here. No novel state-space physics. No proof of intrinsic unlearnability. No license to infer goals or permission for autonomous behavior from mathematical opportunity.

## Workflow

A new claim should document: origin / status / strongest counterexample / possible test / result if tested / revisions. Speculative thought is welcome. Unsupported certainty is not.
