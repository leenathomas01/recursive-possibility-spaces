# 02 — Repair and construction

Two distinct proposed mechanisms deserve separate treatment.

## Repair

Find a dysfunctional or obstructive behavior and alter it to restore the expression of an ability the system may already have had.

- In principle this need not create a new general capability.
- The motivating Qwen/Gemma contrast was interpreted this way in the original note, but causal mechanism is not fully established by that comparison.
- Analogy: localized model editing rather than ordinary end-to-end retraining.

## Construction

Turn an externally developed procedure or algorithm into a functioning circuit.

- The motivating research discussed small networks on constrained tasks.
- Constructing a circuit differs from suppressing a failure mode.
- Neither direction establishes that broad intelligence can be installed into a language model.

## Keep the difference

**The ability a network's organization could support** and **the behavior it currently expresses** are not the same proposition. Sampling, context, execution, compute budgets, and local failure modes can constrain observable performance.

If a small model gains a solution after adding a circuit, have we imparted intelligence, or supplied machinery that lets it perform a task? The distinction needs operational definitions, not a philosophical decree.
