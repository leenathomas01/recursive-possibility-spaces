# Appendix — Heritage collection

**Status:** speculative and lightly humorous.

If admission to an archive does not require immediate utility, historical or unusual computational patterns can be retained even when nobody intends to install them. Examples might include stylistic tendencies, once-useful strategies, curious failure modes, or quirks that later architectures no longer reproduce.

- **Preserved, not installed:** retention for history and inquiry says nothing about suitability for deployment.
- **Restricted alteration:** edits to designated heritage entries require an authorized maintainer.
- **Lineage matters:** derivations should link back to their source artifacts.
- **Permission remains separate:** protected preservation is not a technical claim that the artifact is safe or valuable.
